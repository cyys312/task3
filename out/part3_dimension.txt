  Moran's equation  sum_i s_i^D = 1  does NOT apply here:
  it requires every map to be a similarity (sigma_1 = sigma_2),
  and f3, f4 contract by different amounts in different
  directions.  The fern is self-AFFINE, not self-SIMILAR.

  The correct generalisation is Falconer's singular value
  function  phi^s(A) = sigma_1 * sigma_2^(s-1),  solved for
  sum_i phi^s(A_i) = 1.  Falconer (1988) proves this is an
  UPPER BOUND on the box dimension, attained for almost every
  choice of translation vectors.

  ==> Falconer affinity dimension  =  1.8433   (upper bound)
